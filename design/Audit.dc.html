Consistency audit
What was found, and what changed
Already correct — left alone
Contrast already met WCAG AA on both surfaces. Brand cyan was already confined to rules and bars, never text. Emphasis weights were already consistent at 600 and 700. The direction: rtl on the folder path is a deliberate trick to keep the tail of a long path visible — documented, not "fixed".
1
One block sat off the page grid
The employee strip put its second field at MARGIN + 300 = 342pt, while the page's right column axis is 308.64pt.
342.00 → 322.64  one COL_PITCH from column one
2
Thirteen type sizes, four doing one job
Uppercase micro-labels were set at 6.4, 6.5, 6.8 and 7 — each call site chose its own. Rows were 8.4 and their totals 8.6, a difference nobody can see, where the weight already carried the distinction.
13 sizes → 6 named roles
3
The two surfaces disagreed on a colour
The hairline was #E2E8ED in the stylesheet and #D8DEE4 in the PDF: one role, two values, nothing watching. Colour is now decided once and generated into the stylesheet.
2 hand-kept lists → theme.PALETTE → tokens.css
4
A long job title would have escaped its band
The band was a fixed 62pt while the designation was drawn at top + 42 with 10.5 leading — a second line landed at 52.5, below the band's edge at 48. Current titles fit on one line, so it had never shown. The band is now sized from the wrapped text.
5
Fonts registered for a design that was never shipped
Palatino and Georgia were loaded at startup for two rejected candidates and never drawn again — so a machine without Georgia crashed on a font the payslip does not use. Removed, along with three unused colour tokens.
